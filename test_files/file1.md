# Hansel and Gretel (Summary)

Hansel and Gretel were a young brother and sister who got lost in the dark forest. After wandering for a long time, they found a strange house made entirely of candy, chocolate, and gingerbread.

Unfortunately, an evil witch lived there and she wanted to eat them. She locked Hansel up and made Gretel do chores. Eventually, Gretel tricked the witch, pushed her into the hot oven, and the siblings escaped. They found their way back home to their father and lived happily.

# Goldilocks (Brief Version)

A young girl named Goldilocks wandered into the forest and entered the empty house of three bears while they were out for a walk.

She ate their porridge, broke one of their chairs by sitting on it, and eventually fell asleep in one of the beds upstairs. When the bears returned, they realized someone had been in their house. They found her sleeping. Goldilocks woke up, got very scared of the bears, and ran away into the forest, never to return.
