# The Complete Story of Hansel and Gretel

Hansel and Gretel lived with their poor woodcutter father and their cruel stepmother. During a terrible famine, the stepmother convinced the father to abandon the children in the forest because they didn't have enough food.

The first time they were left in the woods, Hansel dropped **white pebbles** that shone in the moonlight to find the way back. The second time, he used **breadcrumbs**, but the forest birds ate them, leaving the children truly lost.

Wandering for three days, they discovered a magical cottage built of **gingerbread, cakes, and clear sugar windows**. The owner was a wicked witch with very poor eyesight. She locked Hansel in an iron cage to fatten him up, forcing Gretel to do hard chores. Every day, the witch checked Hansel's finger to see if he was getting fat, but Hansel tricked her by holding out a **small chicken bone**.

Eventually, the witch lost patience and ordered Gretel to heat the great oven. Gretel feigned ignorance, asking the witch to show her how to look inside. When the witch leaned into the oven, Gretel shoved her inside and locked the heavy iron door.

The children found chests filled with **pearls and precious stones** in the witch's house. On their way home, a **magical white duck** helped them cross a wide river. They returned to their father, discovering the stepmother had passed away, and they never went hungry again.

# Goldilocks and the Three Bears: Full Details

Once upon a time, there were three bears who lived in a cozy house in the woods: a great big **Papa Bear**, a middle-sized **Mama Bear**, and a tiny little **Baby Bear**. One morning, Mama Bear made oatmeal porridge, but it was too hot to eat, so they went for a walk in the woods while it cooled.

A curious girl named Goldilocks with bright blonde hair found their house. She tasted Papa Bear's porridge, which was **too hot**. Mama Bear's was **too cold**. But Baby Bear's porridge was **just right**, and she ate it all up.

Next, she sat in Papa Bear's massive chair, which was **too hard**. Mama Bear's chair was **too soft**. Baby Bear's chair was **just right**, but she sat down so heavily that it broke into pieces on the floor.

Tired, she went upstairs to the bedroom. Papa Bear's bed was too high at the head, Mama Bear's was too high at the foot, but Baby Bear's bed was perfectly comfortable, and she fell fast asleep.

The bears returned. Papa Bear growled in his deep voice, "Somebody has been eating my porridge!" Baby Bear cried, "Somebody ate all my porridge and broke my chair!" Upstairs, Baby Bear squeaked, "Somebody is sleeping in my bed!" Goldilocks woke up, saw the three angry bears, jumped out the window, and sprinted away as fast as her legs could carry her.
